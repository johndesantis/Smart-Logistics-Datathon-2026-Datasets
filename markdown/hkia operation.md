# Cargo Import and Export Operations at Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA)

## 📥 Import Cargo Process
1. **Unloading and Transfer**: Inbound cargo is initially processed by an autoloader, which transfers the goods to a crane truck for handling.
2. **Quality Inspection**: Once loaded onto the truck, the cargo undergoes its initial quality and safety checks.
3. **Specialized Storage**: The goods are then transported to storage facilities. These rooms feature specific, temperature-controlled environments tailored to different product types to preserve their optimal quality.

## 📤 Export Cargo Process
1. **Specialized Handling**: Hazardous and flammable cargoes are handled separately using designated blue and red cranes to ensure strict safety compliance.
2. **Loading and Security Screening**: Standard outbound cargo is lifted by an autoloader and sent for mandatory quality and security checks, primarily utilizing X-ray scanning.
3. **Dispatch to Aircraft**: Once cleared by security, the cargo is loaded onto trucks and transported directly to the airplane for departure.

## ⚠️ Exception Handling
* **Overweight Cargo**: Excessively heavy shipments must undergo several additional quality and safety inspections before they are cleared for final loading.
* **Missing Documentation**: If a shipment has incomplete or missing information, the cargo operator will temporarily hold the goods and proactively contact the importer to clarify the details.

## 🐾 Special Cargo: Live Animals
* **General Live Animals**: Animals in transit are safely housed in secure cages for a maximum of 6 hours. During this time, they are fed and cared for according to their specific requirements.
* **Equine Transport (Horses)**: Horses are accommodated in a large, specifically controlled field or facility within the airport where they can receive additional feeding and care. From there, they are prepared for international transport (e.g., exports to Australia or mainland China, and imports from regions such as Xinjiang).
